listly stores data on this device

Your login, your saved checklists and any finished checklist waiting to upload are kept in this browser's storage. That is what lets the app work with no internet. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no third party can read it.

Stored here: who you are logged in as, your colour theme, checklists you have saved, answers part-way through, and submissions that have not uploaded yet.
